# Thafari 🦁🌍

## Discover Africa. Experience the Journey.

Thafari is a modern travel and safari booking platform designed to make it easier for travellers to discover tours, plan trips, make bookings, make payments, and communicate with tour operators.

It also gives tour operators and administrators tools to manage tours, bookings, payments, customers, and day-to-day travel operations from one platform.

---

## What is Thafari?

Thafari connects **travellers, tour operators, and administrators** in one travel platform.

### Travellers can

- Create an account
- Browse available tours and safaris
- View detailed tour information
- View departure information
- Make bookings
- Make payments
- Receive booking and payment notifications
- Communicate with tour operators
- Request cancellations where applicable
- Track booking updates

### Tour operators can

- Create and manage tours
- Add tour information and images
- Manage itineraries
- Add accommodation information
- Add frequently asked questions for their tours
- Manage departures
- View and manage customer bookings
- Communicate with customers
- Review relevant payment and booking activity

### Administrators can

- Manage users and user roles
- Manage tours
- Monitor bookings
- Review direct payments
- Manage cancellation and refund processes
- View business intelligence and performance information
- Manage the wider operation of the platform
- Communicate with users when needed

---

## The Main Goal

The goal of Thafari is to bring the important parts of a safari and tour booking journey into one place.

Travellers can discover tours, view information, make bookings, make payments, communicate with operators, and receive updates through the same platform.

Tour operators get tools for managing their tours and interacting with customers.

Administrators get tools for overseeing the platform and its operations.

---

## How a Traveller Uses Thafari

### 1. Discover

The traveller explores available tours and safari experiences.

### 2. Explore a Tour

A tour page can include:

- Destination
- Tour description
- Price
- Departure information
- Itinerary
- Accommodation
- Frequently asked questions
- Images

### 3. Book

The traveller selects the relevant departure and number of people and creates a booking.

### 4. Pay

The traveller proceeds through the available payment process.

Thafari supports payment workflows including direct payment verification and online payment processing.

### 5. Receive Updates

The traveller receives notifications about important booking and payment events.

### 6. Communicate

The traveller can communicate with the relevant tour operator through the in-app messaging system.

### 7. Manage the Booking

Depending on the booking status and applicable process, the traveller can view updates and request cancellation or support.

---

## Tour Operator Experience

Tour operators have their own workspace within Thafari.

They can manage information that travellers see about their tours, including:

- Tour name
- Destination
- Price
- Departure information
- Cover image
- Gallery images
- Itinerary
- Accommodation
- Frequently asked questions

They can also manage customer-related activities connected to their tours.

---

## Administration

Thafari includes an administration area for managing the platform.

Administrators can work with:

- Users
- Customer accounts
- Tour operators
- Tours
- Bookings
- Payments
- Cancellations
- Refunds
- Notifications
- Messages
- Business intelligence

Administrators can also change a user's role between **Customer** and **Tour Operator** where appropriate.

---

## Payments

Thafari includes payment workflows connected to the booking process.

Payment activity can be associated with bookings so the platform can track the relationship between:

**Customer → Booking → Payment**

Direct payments can be reviewed by authorized staff before a booking is confirmed.

The platform also supports an online payment workflow.

---

## Booking Statuses

Bookings can move through different stages:

### Pending

The booking has been created but has not yet been fully confirmed.

### Confirmed

The booking has successfully been confirmed.

### Cancelled

The booking has been cancelled.

### Expired

The booking was not confirmed within the required period and has expired.

### Completed

The trip or booking has been completed.

---

## Notifications

Thafari includes an in-app notification system for important events such as:

- Booking updates
- Payment updates
- Refunds
- Messages
- Departures

Users can open relevant notifications and, where applicable, go directly to the related part of the platform.

---

## Messaging

Thafari includes an in-app chat system that allows customers and tour operators to communicate.

The messaging system is designed around the relationship between a customer and a tour operator rather than creating a separate conversation for every booking.

---

## Business Intelligence

Thafari includes a Business Intelligence area for administrators.

It provides information about areas such as:

- Revenue
- Customers
- Bookings
- Tour performance
- Business targets
- Operational activity

The purpose is to turn platform activity into information that can support business monitoring and decision-making.

---

## User Roles

### Customer

A traveller who uses Thafari to discover tours, make bookings, make payments, and communicate with tour operators.

### Tour Operator

A person or business responsible for providing and managing tours on the platform.

### Administrator

A platform administrator responsible for managing users, tours, bookings, payments, and other administrative activities.

## Technology

Thafari is a full-stack web application.

### Frontend

- React
- Vite
- React Router
- JavaScript
- CSS

### Backend

- Python
- Flask
- Flask extensions for authentication, database management, email, and real-time communication

### Database

- MySQL
- SQLAlchemy
